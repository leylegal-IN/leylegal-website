In short

  • When you join the waitlist we keep your name, your email, your WhatsApp number if you choose to give it, whether you are an advocate or a law student, and when you joined.
  • We use them only to tell you when your access to Ley Legal opens. We don’t sell them or use them for advertising.
  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete them at any time.

01Who we are

Ley Legal (leylegal.in) is run by [legal name: Bhanu Sinha, or Ley Legal Private Limited once registered], [postal address]. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Ley Legal. Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, we are the “Data Fiduciary” for the details you give us.

This policy covers this website and its waitlist. The Ley Legal research platform will have its own terms and privacy policy when it opens. Ley Legal is a research tool for advocates and law students. It does not give legal advice.

02What we collect

When you join the waitlist:

  • your name
  • your email address
  • your WhatsApp number, only if you choose to add it
  • whether you are an advocate or a law student
  • the date and time you joined

That is all. The WhatsApp number is optional. We don’t ask for your address, enrolment number or anything about your cases.

When you visit the site: like any website, our hosting provider records technical details of each visit: your IP address, browser type, the page you asked for and the time. These records keep the site working and secure. The site’s fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so Google also receives your IP address and browser details.

The site sets no cookies and uses no advertising trackers or analytics. If you switch the site to Hindi, your browser remembers that choice on your own device. It is not sent to us.

03Why we use it

  • To email you, and to message you on WhatsApp if you gave your number, when your access to Ley Legal opens.
  • To know how many advocates and law students are waiting, so we can plan the pilot.

We use your details on the basis of your consent, which you give by submitting the waitlist form. We won’t email or message you about anything else, and we never sell or rent your details.

07Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • ask what we hold about you and how we use it
  • correct or update your details
  • have them deleted and leave the waitlist
  • withdraw your consent at any time. It is as easy as giving it: just email us.
  • nominate someone to use these rights for you if you die or cannot act yourself
  • raise a complaint with us, and if you are not satisfied with our reply, with the Data Protection Board of India

To do any of these, write to [privacy@leylegal.in] from the email address you signed up with. We will reply within [number] days.

This policy is also available in Hindi, or in any other language in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution, on request.

08How we protect it

The site only uses encrypted connections (HTTPS). The waitlist is reached only through our own server code, never directly from your browser, and downloading the list needs a password. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your details, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board as the law requires.

09Children

The waitlist is for advocates and law students. It is not meant for anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has signed up, we will delete their details.
